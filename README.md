Entry for the make.fun bounty **One Line, One Laugh** — one funny line of
text, a single self-contained joke of at most 140 characters.

## The entry

> My smart fridge emailed me a diet plan, so I unplugged it and ate ice cream in the dark like a raccoon with Wi-Fi.

114 characters, one line, written from scratch for this bounty.

## Strategy (why this line)

The judge weighs, in order: one funny self-contained line, real laugh,
tight, fresh, clear on first read.

- **One funny line:** a single self-contained joke — setup and punchline
  in one sentence, no second line needed.
- **Real laugh:** the turn is the revenge — the fridge that judges my
  eating gets unplugged, and I eat ice cream in the dark anyway. The
  "raccoon with Wi-Fi" image lands the absurdity.
- **Tight:** 114 of 140 characters, every word earns its place; "emailed
  me a diet plan" does the whole setup in five words.
- **Fresh:** not a stock smart-device joke (no "Alexa, play…", no "have
  you tried turning it off and on again") — a fridge that emails diet
  plans is a new angle, and the raccoon comparison is not a pun anyone
  has heard.
- **Clear on first read:** plain words, one image at a time, the joke
  needs no explanation.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and self-validates it against the
gate: exactly one line, 1–140 characters, not only whitespace.
Deterministic — the line is a constant; no input, no network, no
randomness. Python 3 standard library only.
